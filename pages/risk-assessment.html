    How Risk Assessment Works

    The risk score is generated from attendance and, when Numeric grading applies, numeric grade records. It is an early warning guide for teacher support, not a final judgment of the learner.

    Descriptive grading: Grade 1 is excluded from academic grade-risk scoring. Grades 2–3 are also excluded when the school-year Elementary Report Card Grading Mode is set to Descriptive. Attendance risk remains active; descriptive ratings are not treated as missing numeric grades.

    Areas Checked

    Area Checked What the System Looks For Risk Signal
    Attendance Learner has many absences during the grading period. Frequent or chronic absence
    Tardiness Learner has repeated late or cutting records. Repeated tardiness
    Numeric Grade Completion For Numeric grading only, some or all expected subject grades are not yet encoded. Missing or incomplete grades
    Numeric Subject Performance For Numeric grading only, one or more subject grades are below 75. Failing subject/s
    Numeric Period Average For Numeric grading only, the learner's average for the grading period is below 75. Low period performance
    Numeric General Average For Numeric grading only, the learner's computed general average is below 75. Low overall performance
    Numeric Subject Trend For Numeric grading only, after the first grading period a subject grade drops by 5 or more points from the previous quarter. Sudden subject grade drop
    Numeric Continuous Decline For Numeric grading only, after the first grading period the same subject or average keeps decreasing across grading periods. Continuous grade decline

    Risk Level Guide

    Score Level Meaning Suggested Action
    0-39 Low No major warning signs detected. Continue routine monitoring.
    40-59 Medium Learner shows one or more early warning signs. Provide targeted support and monitor next grading period.
    60-100 High Learner needs urgent attention. Adviser follow-up, parent contact, and documented intervention.

    Score Components

    Component Max Points Examples
    Attendance 40 Absences, repeated tardiness
    Academic Performance 40 Numeric grading only: failing subject, low average, or missing grades; excluded in Descriptive mode.
    Grade Trend 20 Sudden drops or repeated decline
    Total 100 Final risk score
    First Grading Period 50 / 50 / 0 Attendance and academic performance only; grade trend is disabled until a previous period exists

    Risk flags are recomputed from live records. If attendance or grades are corrected, the risk level and indicators may change after the assessment is refreshed.
